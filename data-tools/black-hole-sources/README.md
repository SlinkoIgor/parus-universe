# Published compact-object inputs

These compact factual rows reproduce the additive catalogue offline. They are
historical source snapshots, not a claim of a complete present-day census.
URLs, retrieval date and available source hashes are in each JSON file.

- `blackcat-2015.json`: all 59 objects of arXiv:1510.08869v1. Coordinates and
  distance from source `table2.tex`, individual mass field from `table5.tex`.
  RA is hours/minutes/seconds, Dec degrees/arcminutes/arcseconds (J2000), distance
  kpc, mass M_sun. Raw bounds, intervals, LaTeX uncertainties and reference keys
  stay raw; grey source rows identify 18 dynamical systems. All 18 have their
  corresponding mass-table field, which may itself be only a bound or interval.
  Identity matching used all aliases and normalized spaces, punctuation and an
  optional J after the 1H prefix. Thus `1H 1659-487` and `1H J1659-487` match
  through a general source-identity normalization, not a numerical mass guess.
- `smbh-2013.json`: Table 2, both displayed halves of arXiv:1211.2816v3;
  73 estimates, 72 unique hosts. Mass and its asymmetric (+,-) errors use M_sun,
  including the displayed exponential multiplier; D uses Mpc. Column names,
  original cell strings, method and reference identifiers are retained. The
  two N1399 solutions are distinct measurements, not repeated rows to average.
- `gaia.json`: RA/Dec degrees, parallax mas, mass M_sun, quoted uncertainties
  and reference epoch. BH1/BH2 use the adopted joint solutions of their source
  tables; BH3 uses the joint solution in Table 2/Eq.5 of arXiv:2404.10486.

The runtime data and audit are generated by
`node data-tools/build-black-hole-catalog.mjs` from the repository root.
The builder preserves original raw rows, attaches explicit numerical units,
selects usable distances without averaging bounds, joins full host identities,
and records the adopted-distance mass scale. Source PDFs, imagery, spectra,
article prose and download archives are not redistributed. Coverage/selection
and attribution: ../../docs/atlas-expansion.md and ../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.
